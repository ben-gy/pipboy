Hidden controls: press ~ for the developer console (try tgm, tcl, showracemenu, coc qasmoke, player.additem caps 1000, goto 22, wildwasteland). Press Ctrl+Shift+P to cycle phosphor color. There is also a famous code involving arrows, two letters, and a button.
